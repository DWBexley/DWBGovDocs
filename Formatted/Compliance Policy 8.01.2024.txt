DEL WEBB BEXLEY COMMUNITY ASSOCIATION, INC. RESOLUTION ADOPTING COMPLIANCE POLICY

WHEREAS, Del Webb Bexley Community Association, Inc. (the "Association") is a Florida not-for-profit corporation; and  

WHEREAS, the Association is a homeowners association governed by Chapter 720, Florida Statutes, and its governing documents, including the Articles of Incorporation, Bylaws, and the Community Declaration; and  

WHEREAS, Florida Statutes and the Association's governing documents authorize the Board of Directors to levy fines and suspend use rights for violations of the governing documents; and  

WHEREAS, the Board has the authority to make and enforce rules and regulations governing the community.  

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED, that the Board of Directors hereby approves and adopts the Compliance Policy attached as Exhibit A.  

This resolution was adopted by the Board of Directors on August 15, 2024.  

    Signed by: Gary Zima, President.  

EXHIBIT A: COMPLIANCE POLICY

I. Association Remedies for Non-Compliance

    Fines and Suspensions: Fines may be levied up to $100.00 per day, per violation.  

    Approval Process: Fines will not be imposed until approved by the Compliance Committee following a noticed hearing.  

    No Cap: Pursuant to the Declaration, aggregate fines for continuing violations are not capped.  

    Common Area Suspension: The Association may suspend the use of common areas and facilities for violations.  

    Payment Terms: Fines must be paid within thirty (30) days of the notice.  

    Legal Remedies: The Association reserves the right to pursue other remedies, including litigation or mediation, regardless of fines imposed.  

II. Procedures for Notifications and Cure

    1st Notice to Owner: Sent via US Mail and community portal email.  

        Includes a description of the violation and a request for correction within a specified time (not less than 7 days).  

        Owners may request an extension in writing before the deadline.  

    Fine/Suspension Notice: Issued if the violation is not corrected.  

        Provides notice of a Compliance Committee hearing scheduled between 14 and 90 days after the notice.  

        If the violation is cured before the hearing, the fine or suspension may not be imposed.  

    Notice of Decision: Sent within 7 days of the hearing, detailing the committee's findings and any approved fines or suspensions.  

III. Inspections and Responses

    Owner Responsibility: Owners must provide accurate contact information and notify the Association in writing of address changes.  

    Reporting Cure: Owners must notify the Property Manager via the portal when a violation is corrected; the manager will سپس conduct a validation inspection.  

    Assistance: Homeowners needing help with the portal must schedule an appointment with the property manager.  

IV. Categories of Violations and Fines

The policy defines three categories of violations:  

Category: C1	
Description: Incident-related (should not be repeated)
Examples: Trash cans visible, blocking sidewalk, overnight street parking, unleashed dogs, pet waste	
Fines/Actions: $100 per day for 1st, 2nd, and 3rd violations

Category: C2	
Description: Occurs over time (takes time to correct)
Examples: Dirty roofs/driveways, exterior painting needed, shutter repair	
Fines/Actions: $100 per day; 1st notice allows 45 days to enter a contract

Category: C3	
Description: Consequential/Disruptive behavior
Examples: Safety violations, ARC rules, disruptive behavior at the clubhouse/pool	
Fines/Actions: $100 per day + possible suspension
