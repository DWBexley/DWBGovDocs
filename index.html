HOA Document "Cheat Sheet"

To get the most accurate answers about our community rules, follow these 3 simple steps to let AI (Gemini) read the entire document for you.

1

Save the Document

Click the button below to open the full document. Once it opens, Right-Click anywhere on the text and choose "Save As..." to save it to your computer (Desktop or Downloads folder).

Open HOA Document
2

Open Gemini

Go to the Gemini website. You may need to sign in with your Google/Gmail account.

Open Gemini AI
3

Upload & Ask

In the Gemini chat box at the bottom:

  • Look for the Plus (+) or Paperclip icon.
  • Select the HOADocs.txt file you just saved.
  • Type your question and press Enter!
Try asking these questions:
  • "Based on this file, what are the rules for pets and golf carts?"
  • "Can you make a table of all the amendments and what they changed?"
  • "I want to install a pool enclosure. What is the process?"

Note: This tool is designed to help you find information quickly. Always verify official legal matters with the Board.
