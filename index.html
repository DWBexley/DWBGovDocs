HOA Document AI Helper

Follow these steps to allow Gemini AI to analyze the complete community document for you.

1

Download the Master File

First, click the blue button below to open the document. Once the page of text opens, use the Quick Keys for your computer to save it:

Open HOA Document
Windows PCs:
Press Ctrl + S
Apple Macs:
Press Command ⌘ + S

Save the file as "HOADocs.txt" to your Desktop or Downloads folder.

2

Open Gemini

Click below to open the Google Gemini website. (You may need to log in with your Google or Gmail account).

Go to Gemini AI
3

Upload and Ask

In the Gemini "Type something here" box at the bottom:

  1. Click the Plus (+) or Paperclip icon.
  2. Choose the HOADocs.txt file you just saved.
  3. Ask your question (e.g., "What are the rules for fences?") and press Enter.
Helpful Questions to Try:
  • "Summarize the 2018 Second Amendment regarding pets."
  • "What is the process for getting a pool enclosure approved?"
  • "Create a list of all amendments and what they changed."
